Crispy Halloumi Bowls

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 package halloumi (7–8 oz)
  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 2 cups romaine lettuce
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1/4 red onion
  • 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley
  • 1 tsp fresh dill
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

Step 1: Preheat oven to 425°F. Cut potatoes into wedges and toss with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.

Step 2: Season chicken breasts with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper.

Step 3: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook chicken for 5–7 minutes per side.

Step 4: While the chicken cooks, chop romaine lettuce, tomato, cucumber, red onion, and parsley. Toss together with a squeeze of lemon juice.

Step 5: Make the tzatziki by mixing Greek yogurt, grated cucumber (squeeze out excess water), minced garlic, lemon juice, fresh dill, salt, and pepper.

Step 6: Slice halloumi into thick strips. Heat a little olive oil in a pan over medium heat and cook for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and crispy. Turn off heat, drizzle with honey, and toss to coat.

Step 7: Assemble bowls with chopped salad, roasted potatoes, sliced chicken, and crispy honey halloumi. Finish with a generous spoonful of tzatziki.
