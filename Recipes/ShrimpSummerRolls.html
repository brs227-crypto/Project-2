Shrimp Summer Rolls

Prep Time
15mins
Cook Time
5mins
Total Time
20mins
Servings
2 (4 Rolls)

Ingredients

  • 12 large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 rice paper wrappers
  • ½ cucumber
  • 1 carrot
  • ½ cup shredded purple cabbage
  • ½ cup lettuce
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp paprika
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Directions

Step 1: Season shrimp with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.

Step 2: Heat a pan over medium heat and cook shrimp for 2–3 minutes per side until pink, opaque, and cooked through.

Step 3: Thinly slice cucumber and carrot into strips. Shred cabbage and chop lettuce.

Step 4: Fill a shallow bowl with warm water. Dip one rice paper wrapper into the water for a few seconds until it begins to soften.

Step 5: Lay the wrapper on a flat surface. Add 3 shrimp, cucumber, carrot, cabbage, and lettuce in the center.

Step 6: Fold in the sides and roll tightly, like a burrito. Repeat with remaining wrappers.

Step 7: Serve fresh with your favorite dipping sauce.
